# English Speaking Practice Notes

## 1. 洗漱完以后

**After I finished getting ready, ...**

**After I washed up, ...**

其中 **get ready** 范围更广，不只是“洗漱”，还可以包括刷牙、洗脸、换衣服、整理自己等。

例如：

> After I finished getting ready, I grabbed my bag and headed out.

---

## 2. 擦一点面霜

**I put on some face cream.**

**I put on a little face cream.**

其中 **put on** 不仅可以表示“穿衣服”，也可以表示“涂 / 擦”护肤品。

例如：

> I brushed my teeth, washed my face, and put on some face cream.

---

## 3. 刷牙

**brush my teeth**

例如：

> I brushed my teeth and washed my face.

注意：

- ❌ wash my teeth
- ✅ brush my teeth

---

## 4. 出门

**head out**

例如：

> I grab my bag, lock the door, and head out.

注意区分：

- **head out** = 出门 / 出发
- **hand out** = 分发

例如：

> The teacher handed out the papers.

---

## 5. 下楼

**head downstairs**

也可以说：

**go downstairs**

如果特别强调“走楼梯下去”：

**walk down the stairs**

例如：

> Then I head downstairs since I live on the fifth floor.

---

## 6. 我住在五楼

**I live on the fifth floor.**

楼层前通常使用 **on**：

> I live on the fifth floor.

> My office is on the 10th floor.

注意：

- five → **fifth**
- fifteen → **fifteenth**

但是地铁线路说：

**Line 13**

而不是：

**the thirteenth line**

---

## 7. 出小区

**leave the complex**

例如：

> Then I leave the complex.

更完整可以说：

**residential complex**

在上下文明确的情况下，直接说 **the complex** 就可以。

---

## 8. 共享单车 / 扫一辆共享单车

**shared bike**

例如：

> I scan a HelloBike.

如果需要向别人解释 HelloBike：

> I scan a HelloBike, a shared bike.

注意：

- ❌ share bike
- ✅ shared bike

---

## 9. 骑车大约五分钟

**I ride the bike for about five minutes.**

更自然地表达“需要五分钟”：

**It takes me about five minutes.**

例如：

> I ride the bike to the subway station. It takes me about five minutes.

表示一段持续时间时，经常使用：

**for + 一段时间**

例如：

> I wait for ten minutes.

> I walk for twenty minutes.

> I ride the bike for about five minutes.

---

## 10. 到达地铁站 / 到达办公室

**arrive at the subway station**

**arrive at my office**

例如：

> I arrive at the subway station.

> Then I arrive at my office.

日常口语中，“到公司”还可以更自然地说：

**get to the office**

例如：

> Then I ride three more stops and get to the office.

---

## 11. 坐五站

**ride five stops**

例如：

> I ride five stops.

> I ride five stops and transfer to Line 15.

地铁的“站数”可以使用 **stop**：

- one stop
- three stops
- five stops

---

## 12. 换乘 15 号线

**transfer to Line 15**

例如：

> I ride five stops and transfer to Line 15.

记住完整搭配：

**transfer to + 线路**

例如：

> I transfer to Line 15.

线路通常直接说：

- **Line 13**
- **Line 15**

而不是：

- the Line 13
- the Line 15

---

## 13. 再坐三站

**ride three more stops**

例如：

> I transfer to Line 15 and ride three more stops.

常用语序：

**数字 + more + 名词**

例如：

> three more stops

> two more minutes

> five more days

> one more question

---

## 14. 描述日常通勤

如果是在描述每天的习惯，使用一般现在时：

> I leave the complex.

> I ride a bike to the subway station.

> I take the subway to my office.

> I start on Line 13.

> I ride five stops and transfer to Line 15.

> I ride three more stops and arrive at my office.

如果是在讲“今天早上发生的事情”，使用过去时：

> I left the complex.

> I rode a bike to the subway station.

> I took the subway to my office.

> I started on Line 13.

> I rode five stops and transferred to Line 15.

> I rode three more stops and arrived at my office.

同一段叙述中要尽量保持时态一致。

---

## 15. 我不太懂你的意思

**Sorry, I don't quite get what you mean.**

更简单也可以说：

**I don't get what you mean.**

其中：

**I don't quite get it.**

可以理解为：

**我不太明白 / 我没有完全理解。**

例如：

> Sorry, I don't quite get what you mean.

---

## 16. 你可以重复一遍吗？

日常口语：

**Sorry, can you say that again?**

稍微正式一点：

**Could you repeat that, please?**

如果是因为没有听清楚：

**Sorry, I couldn't hear you clearly.**

如果听到了，但是没有理解：

**Sorry, I don't quite get what you mean.**

可以区分：

- **Sorry, can you say that again?** → 可以再说一遍吗？
- **Sorry, I couldn't hear you clearly.** → 我刚刚没听清。
- **Sorry, I don't quite get what you mean.** → 我不太理解你的意思。

---

# Full Example: My Morning Commute

> After I finish getting ready, I grab my bag, lock the door, and head out.
>
> Then I head downstairs since I live on the fifth floor.
>
> After I leave the complex, I scan a HelloBike and ride it to the subway station. It takes me about five minutes.
>
> Then I take the subway to my office. I start on Line 13, ride five stops, transfer to Line 15, ride three more stops, and arrive at my office.
